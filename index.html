Our Story

A little bit of Melbourne
in the heart of Kenmore.

Owned by Reagan Nongkhlaw and Urvik Bhalani, Method to the Madness was born from a love of great food, great coffee, and the kind of welcome that makes you feel like a regular — even on your first visit.

Inspired by Shakespeare and shaped by Melbourne, we've built a light-filled laneway cafe where the menu is as creative as the quotes on the walls. Come for the hotcakes, stay for the brisket burger.
