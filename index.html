Act I · The Story

A Stage
Like No Other.

❖

Tucked down Marshall Lane in Kenmore, Method to the Madness is Brisbane’s most literary café. The name is Shakespeare. The soul is Melbourne laneway. The food is world-class.

Reagan Nongkhlaw and Urvik Bhalani built a space where the Bard’s best lines share walls with Brisbane’s best brunch — Shakespearean quotes, a shelf of his works, and a light-filled laneway that feels like a stage set.

From Breakfast Tacos to Beef Brisket Burgers, from boozy brunch cocktails to a menu that shifts with the seasons — every plate is, as Will himself would say, a thing of beauty most rare.

Scene I

All Day Breakfast

Iconic

Strawberry & Matcha Hotcakes

Japanese-inspired stacked hotcakes with fresh strawberries and matcha cream — sweet as a sonnet.

Fiery

Chili Scrambled Eggs

Soft scrambled eggs with chili oil, fresh herbs, and crusty sourdough. A tragedy of flavour.

Fan Favourite

Breakfast Tacos

Soft tacos loaded with breakfast fillings, house salsa, and crema. “To eat, or not to eat” is no question here.

Pumpkin & Halloumi Fritters

Golden fritters with charred halloumi, roasted pumpkin, and herbed yogurt — a comedy of textures.

Mumbai Omelette

Spiced Indian-style omelette with masala filling and house chutney. East meets West End.

Fig & Apple Bircher

Overnight oats with fresh fig, crisp apple, toasted nuts, and honey. As quiet as a soliloquy.

Scene II

Luncheon from 10AM

Legendary

Beef Brisket Burger

Slow-cooked brisket, house pickles, slaw, and smoked aioli on a brioche bun. Worth every act of waiting.

Bold

Middle Eastern Pulled Lamb

Fragrant slow-pulled lamb with tahini, pomegranate, and warm flatbread. A thousand nights in one dish.

Samosa Chaat

Crispy samosas on spiced chickpeas with tamarind, yogurt, and coriander. A comedy of cultures.

Marinara Linguine

Fresh seafood in a rich tomato sauce with linguine, chili, and parsley. “What’s in a name? That which we call a prawn…”
