THE STORY

SERIOUS FOOD.
ZERO PRETENSION.

Tucked down Marshall Lane in Kenmore, Method to the Madness is Brisbane's most unexpected café. Think Shakespeare meets Melbourne cool — a laneway vibe, world-class coffee, and a menu that refuses to stay in its lane.

From Breakfast Tacos to Beef Brisket Burgers, Shakespearean quotes on the walls to boozy brunch cocktails — this is where the west side eats well.

Owned by Reagan Nongkhlaw and Urvik Bhalani. Built for people who take good food seriously but themselves not at all.
